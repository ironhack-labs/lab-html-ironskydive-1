How do we structure the day?

1. Training

All Ironhackers need to do a lot of exercises daily, regardless of their dietary preferences in beer and pizza. After all, you want to look like a ninja, not a clumsy bag of potatoes, right? The best thing of all is that in IronSkydive you don't need to lift weights because the parachute will do that for you.

2. Get ready

You're almost ready for your first time flying! The only thing left to do is to suit up and make sure you're fit physically. Fitness is important to us, and technology training.

3. Fly

It's time to take that jump! You're in the best hands — with our experienced instructors. Skydiving is our passion.

4. Jump!

Done with your parachute training? Awesome! Jump many times and practice as long as you want — for this price you already have a pack of 5 jumps. Don't forget your parachute though.
